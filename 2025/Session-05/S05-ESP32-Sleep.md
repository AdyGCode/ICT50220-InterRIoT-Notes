# Sleep Modes and IoT Devices

Many different types of sleep mode. They all have different levels of "deactivation" of the MCU/MPU or other system.

Some common names include:
- Sleep
- Deep Sleep
- Hibernate


ESP32 Sleep Modes

- Light Sleep
- Deep Sleep
