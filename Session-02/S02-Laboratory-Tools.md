# Laboratory Tools

There is a large variety of lab/testing tools.

Lab tools include:
- Oscilloscope: measure and visualise time-based signals
- Signal generator: generate signals, like sine waves
- Power supply: to power any devices that need testing
- Spectrum analyser: to analyse signals in frequency domain
- Multimeter: used to measure various electrical properties
- and others.

### Oscilloscope: what they measure
- Timing characteristics:
    - Frequency and period
    - Duty cycle
    - Phase
    - Rise and fall times

- Voltage characteristics:
    - Amplitude
    - Maximum and minimum voltages
    - Mean and average voltages

## Signal Generators

![](../Pasted%20image%2020250725145532.png)
https://www.tequipment.net/Rigol/DG4102/Function-Generator/

A signal generator generates signals (duh) with various wave forms

- Wave forms include:
    - Sine wave
    - Sawtooth
    - Triangle
    - Block wave
    - Arbitrary (programmable)

- Signal types:
    - Audio (pitch generator)
    - Video


## Power Supplies

![](../Pasted%20image%2020250725145735.png)

 
 A power supply provides the electrical power for other electrical and electronic devices

They may have have multiple outputs.

They are rated for certain voltages and currents:
    - E.g., 30V / 6A


## Spectrum Analysers

![](../Pasted%20image%2020250725145803.png)

A spectrum analyser allows waveforms to be examined in frequency domain

- It measures the power of the spectrum
- Very often used to measure radio signals emitted by an antenna

## Multimeters

![](../Pasted%20image%2020250725145847.png)

- A simple but very versatile tool (digital / analog)
- It measures:
    - Voltage (AC and DC)
    - Current (even up to 10A)
    - Resistance
    - Conductivity

Some multimeters have the capability of measuring capacitors, inductors, diodes and transistors, too.
